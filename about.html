Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about working with Lumina.

How long does a typical project take?
A standard marketing site takes about 2–4 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on scope and content.
Do you provide hosting and domains?
Yes, we can help you set up hosting, domains, email, and analytics as part of a launch package.
What is your pricing model?
We quote each project individually based on scope. You always know the total cost before work begins.
Do you offer ongoing support?
Absolutely. We offer monthly maintenance plans for updates, content changes, and monitoring.
