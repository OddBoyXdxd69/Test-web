Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies on the Lumina website.

Last updated: 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how you use them.

2. Cookies We Use

  • Preference storage: We use your browser's local storage to remember your chosen theme (dark or light). This is stored only on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Strictly necessary: Basic functions of the Site, such as navigation, do not rely on cookies.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We do not currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies on this Site. If that changes, this policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can clear or block local storage and cookies at any time using your browser's settings. Clearing your browser data will reset your saved theme preference to the default.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy, please contact us.
